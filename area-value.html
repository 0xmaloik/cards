Area Ledger

Which areas pay the best, per card drop?

Pulls every area → card mapping from cards.json (deathbeam/maps-of-exile) and live prices from poe.ninja, then ranks areas by the weight-normalized average value of the divination cards that can drop there: Σ(weight×price) ÷ Σweight.

Read before you farm

  • This ranks the expected value of one divination-card drop in an area — not overall map profitability (no mob density, boss kill speed, sextants/scarabs, etc).
  • Areas are internal PoE area IDs (e.g. MapWorldsGraveyard), not the friendly in-game map names.
  • Cards with no listed area (monster/boss-only drops, Rogue Exile cards, etc.) are excluded since they aren't tied to a specific area.
  • poe.ninja prices are player listing prices, not guaranteed sale prices, and fluctuate — re-fetch for the latest snapshot.
  • If a card has no live poe.ninja price, its price from cards.json is used instead (tagged cards.json in the breakdown below each row).
